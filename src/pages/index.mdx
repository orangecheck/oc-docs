# Overview

**orangecheck is one account, with Bitcoin behind it**
([ochk.app](https://ochk.app)). Underneath are six open protocols.

Building an app? [Sign people in](/me/quickstart) with the account: it is free,
and paying your users is optional. Working with the protocols? They share one
canonical message format, one BIP-322 signing primitive and one set of Nostr
conventions. Learn that layer once; each protocol adds a small layer on top.

## The family

Six verbs of sovereign sociality: _am, whisper, decide, declare, delegate,
swear_.

<ProtocolGrid link="docs" className="not-prose my-6 lg:grid-cols-2" />

OC Attest is the **base layer** — every other sibling can optionally reference
an Attest proof as a "stake at signing" signal. The other five siblings are
**peers**: none depends on the others at the protocol level.

    PLEDGE -->|bond| ATTEST
    PLEDGE -->|resolution mechanism| STAMP
    PLEDGE -.->|dispute mechanism| VOTE

    classDef base stroke:#f66d36,stroke-width:2px;

Eight real composition arrows across six protocols: every peer can pin its
artifacts to Attest stake; Pledge resolves through Stamp and disputes through
Vote; every Agent action is itself a Stamp. None of these compositions is
required — but each one is what turns six small protocols into a single
substrate.

## The product and the showcase apps

The protocols are open and free to self-host. On them orangecheck builds one
product, ochk.app, and showcase apps: full, working apps whose other job is to
show a protocol doing real work. They use the same envelopes, the same BIP-322
signing and the same anchor pipeline: consumers of the protocols, not extensions
to them.

| App                      | What it is                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **[ochk.app](/me)**      | One account, with Bitcoin behind it, at [ochk.app](https://ochk.app). Apps that choose to pay their users pay them in sats. orangecheck holds earned sats while payouts are in testing. |
| **[OC Vault](/vault)**   | A password manager that cannot read your data. Secrets are sealed on your device; orangecheck stores ciphertext only.                                                                   |
| **[OC Chat](/chat)**     | End-to-end-encrypted messaging where your Bitcoin address is the inbox. No phone number, and messages open only on your devices.                                                        |
| **[OC Cosign](/cosign)** | Get behind a builder: join as a co-founder, or back them with sats that settle peer-to-peer.                                                                                            |

Each of them operates under the [charter](/charter) — eight commitments that
bind every orangecheck surface (no token, custody you can move out of, the
protocol stays open, audit verifies without us, and so on).

## Conventions

- Short code snippets are runnable as-is. Longer ones link to a full working
  example in
  [`oc-packages/EXAMPLES.md`](https://github.com/orangecheck/oc-packages/blob/main/EXAMPLES.md).
- "Attestation" (lowercase) refers specifically to an **OC Attest** signed
  proof. Lock envelopes, Stamp envelopes, Vote ballots, etc. are not called
  attestations.
- Every protocol ships a spec repo and a reference impl. If the two disagree,
  **the spec is authoritative** and the impl is the bug.
