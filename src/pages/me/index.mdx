# ochk.app

**One account, with Bitcoin behind it.** People sign in the way they already do
(email, Google, GitHub or a Bitcoin wallet) and use the same account in every
app that uses orangecheck. The product lives at [ochk.app](https://ochk.app).
The API, the sign-in popup and the SDK talk to **me.ochk.io**, the API host.

| Sign in                   | Back it                                                                                                                                                            | Get paid                                                      | Own it                                             |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Live. Free for every app. | Live. Bitcoin the owner already holds backs the account, in one coin of at least the amount an app sets (say, 100,000 sats held 30 days), and stays in the wallet. | Optional for apps, in testing. Each paid event has a receipt. | Not open yet. Moving to your own keys is the path. |

**For apps**, sign-in is free and stands on its own. An app can let in every
account, or only accounts **backed by bitcoin**, so bots and farms cost
something real without anyone showing an ID. Paying your users is optional:
always, in promos, or never.

**Custody, stated plainly.** Backing leaves the coin in its owner's wallet.
Earned sats are different: today orangecheck holds them while payouts are in
testing. A federation is next, then keys the person holds. The live state is at
[ochk.app/custody](https://ochk.app/custody) and machine-readable at
`https://me.ochk.io/api/federations`.

## Where the source of truth lives

- **Protocol contracts**: `oc-attest-protocol`, `oc-lock-protocol`,
  `oc-stamp-protocol`, `oc-agent-protocol`. The v1.2-draft-1 federation custody
  extension is at `oc-attest-protocol/FEDERATION-CUSTODY.md`.
- **App**: `oc-app-web` (ochk.app). **API**: `oc-me-web` (me.ochk.io), which
  also serves the sign-in popup.
- **SDK**: `oc-packages/me-client`, published to npm as
  `@orangecheck/me-client`.
- **Docs**: this section, in `oc-docs`.

The contract is the protocol; orangecheck is one implementation. Anyone can run
their own against the same receipts. No token, no airdrop: sats are the unit of
account.
