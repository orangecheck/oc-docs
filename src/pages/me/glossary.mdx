# orangecheck · Glossary

The same word for the same thing, everywhere: ochk.app, ochk.io, these docs and
the SDK. Where the API uses a different name, it is in the last column. API
names do not change.

## For everyone

| Word                                                     | What it means                                                                                                                      | In the API                                                               |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **account**                                              | Your orangecheck account: one account for every app that uses orangecheck.                                                         | `did_oc`, `sub`                                                          |
| **account id**                                           | The account's id. The same in every app, so apps that compare notes can match you.                                                 | `did_oc` (`did:oc:…`)                                                    |
| **ways to sign in**                                      | Email, Google, GitHub or a Bitcoin wallet. A second way in is also your way back.                                                  | linked identities                                                        |
| **back your account** · **backed by bitcoin** · **coin** | You show that you hold one coin, and it backs your account. The coin stays in your wallet. An app can let in only backed accounts. | bond: `oc_bond`, `requireBond`, `bond_required` ([bonds](/attest/bonds)) |
| **the bar**                                              | What an app asks of a backed account: at least N sats, held at least D days.                                                       | floor: `min_sats`, `min_days`                                            |
| **app**                                                  | A service you sign in to with orangecheck.                                                                                         | integrator, site                                                         |
| **event**                                                | One thing you did in an app that pays its users.                                                                                   | billable event, `subtype`                                                |
| **receipt**                                              | The signed record of one event. Anyone can check it offline against Bitcoin.                                                       | envelope                                                                 |
| **Earned · Waiting · Ready to cash out · Paid out**      | Where your sats are: earned, waiting until a date the app set, ready for you to take, or sent.                                     | accrued, vesting / maturing, available, settled                          |
| **Cash out** · **payout**                                | Taking ready sats out to where you choose.                                                                                         | distribution, settlement                                                 |
| **move to your own keys**                                | Holding your sats yourself instead of orangecheck holding them. Not open yet.                                                      | graduation, self-custody                                                 |
| **statements**                                           | What apps say about an account: good, caution, or refused.                                                                         | trust attestations                                                       |
| **what you share**                                       | What you let an app see beyond your account id, item by item. You can take any of it back.                                         | scopes                                                                   |
| **In testing**                                           | Working, with real limits, before it is fully open. Paying users is in testing.                                                    | testnet, beta                                                            |

## For developers

| Word                                      | What it means                                                                                                                       | In the API                                   |
| ----------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| **project**                               | Your app's record on orangecheck: its key, its domain, who can sign in, and whether it pays.                                        | `project_key`, `IntegratorPriceConfig`       |
| **who can sign in**                       | Everyone, or only accounts backed by bitcoin above your bar. [Details](/me/require-bond).                                           | `require_bond`, `projectKey` in `withOcAuth` |
| **Pay your users: Off · Always · Promos** | Whether your events earn your users sats right now. [Details](/me/paying-users).                                                    | pay periods, `pay_periods`, `not_paying`     |
| **Your user earns N sats**                | What one event pays your user. You pay that ÷ 0.8, the 20% fee included: earns 100, you pay 125.                                    | `site_pays`, `user_share_pct`                |
| **One-time · Per action · Per session**   | The three event groups: things done once (signing up), each time (a payment), and every session.                                    | class A · B · C                              |
| **balance** · **Top up**                  | The sats your project holds to pay its users. "About N days left" is how long it lasts at your current rate.                        | escrow, runway                               |
| **Release schedule**                      | When your users' shares are theirs: right away (the default), or in batches, weekly, monthly or when you say. [Details](/me/drops). | drops, drop windows, `drop.closed`           |

A bond backs an account: that is the one place the protocol word meets the
product word. In these docs, "Bitcoin-backed" describes only an account that is
backed.
