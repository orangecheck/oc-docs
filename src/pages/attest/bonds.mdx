# Bonds

An attestation publishes an address, and with it a balance and a history. A
**bond** keeps the coin private. The holder signs once to back one account with
one unspent coin. Apps the person signs in to learn only that a coin of at least
N sats, unspent at least D days, backs them. They also get a number that's the
same for that coin at their app and unrelated everywhere else.

The coin stays in the holder's wallet. Spending it ends the backing. Nobody
needs a bond to sign in: an account made with email, Google, GitHub or a wallet
signs in everywhere without one. Normative spec:
[`BOND.md`](https://github.com/orangecheck/oc-attest-protocol/blob/main/BOND.md).

## The message

```
orangecheck-bond
address: bc1q…
outpoint: <txid>:<vout>
subject: did:oc:…
nonce: <32 hex>
issued_at: 2026-10-16T12:00:00Z
expires_at: 2026-10-16T12:10:00Z
ack: I back this subject with this coin. It stays in my wallet.
```

The coin's own address signs it with BIP-322. `issued_at` and `expires_at` bound
the signing window, at most an hour, not the bond. `bond_id` is the SHA-256 of
the message bytes. The header differs from `orangecheck` and `orangecheck-auth`,
so no signature for one can be replayed as another.

## Verifying

```ts
import { esploraSource, verifyBond } from '@orangecheck/sdk';

const r = await verifyBond({
    message,
    signature,
    subject: didOc,
    source: esploraSource(),
});
// r.ok → r.bond: { bond_id, subject, address, outpoint, sats, confirmed_height, tip }
// else r.reason: sig_invalid | coin_spent | coin_too_recent | coin_address_mismatch | …
```

The coin must be confirmed, at least 6 blocks deep, unspent, and an output of
the signing address. ochk.io also refuses a coin under 1,000 sats, the lowest
floor (`coin_too_small`).

A bond lasts exactly as long as its coin. ochk.io re-checks the coin when its
last check is over ten minutes old, and every claim says when that was.
`checkBondCoin` does that re-check. Python: `orangecheck` ≥ 2.2 has the same
message, id, nullifier and floors.

## One coin, one account

A coin backs one account at a time. The same account can back with it again at
once. Released from one account, it can back a different account only 30 days
later (`coin_backed_another_recently`), the longest a token carrying it can
live, so two accounts never present the same coin at once.

## What an app sees

```json
"oc_bond": {
  "v": 1,
  "sats_floor": 100000,
  "days_floor": 30,
  "height": 921044,
  "checked_at": 1792200000,
  "nullifier": "5b1d…"
}
```

- **Floors, not amounts:** `1000 · 10000 · 100000 · 1000000 · 10000000` sats and
  `7 · 30 · 90 · 365` days, `0` below the lowest.
- **`nullifier`** is
  `HMAC-SHA256(registry key, "orangecheck-bond-nullifier\n" + audience + "\n" + outpoint)`.
  Only ochk.io holds the key, so an app can't run every coin on chain through
  the formula to find the one behind its users. The same coin at your app gives
  the same number, across accounts and re-backing. Another app gets an unrelated
  one. Sites on `*.ochk.io` share one family session, so they share one
  nullifier (audience `https://ochk.io`).
- **`checked_at` and `height`** say when ochk.io last found the coin unspent.
  You can't ask for a fresh check. You can refuse a stale claim:
  `bondMeets(payload, { minSats, minDays, maxAgeSecs })` from
  `@orangecheck/auth-core`.

Keep one account per nullifier. That is the real bot check at your app: a floor
says some coin backs the account, and the nullifier tells you whether that coin
already backed another account of yours.

A registry computes the nullifier with `bondNullifier(key, audience, outpoint)`
from `@orangecheck/sdk` ≥ 1.13. The key comes first, and only the registry has
it.

## Slashing without custody

Ban a nullifier and you've banned the coin. A new account or backing again won't
get past it. Moving the coin will, but a moved coin is new and starts aging from
zero, so it falls below every age floor again. Nothing is confiscated: the coin
stays in the holder's wallet, and abuse costs time.

## Privacy

| Who                    | Learns                                                    |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| The registry (ochk.io) | the address, coin, amount and age; it holds the key       |
| An app                 | floors, when the coin was last checked, its own nullifier |
| The chain              | nothing; the signature is never published                 |

Back with a coin at an address you use for nothing else. A zero-knowledge method
that removes the registry from the picture is future work in the spec (§10).

## Where people back their account

- **ochk.app:** Settings → Back your account with bitcoin.
- **ochk.io:** Dashboard → Identity.
- **me.ochk.io:** Settings → Account.
- **Any family site:** `<OcBond />` from `@orangecheck/auth-client` ≥ 2.30.

To require one at your app, see [Who can sign in](/me/require-bond).
