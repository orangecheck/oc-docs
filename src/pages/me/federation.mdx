# ochk.app · Federation custody

> **Status: this is the design, not today.** No guardian federation is bound.
> [`/api/federations`](https://me.ochk.io/api/federations) is the
> machine-readable answer. It lists two entries, neither with an invite, so no
> client can join one: `oc-me-v1`, recruiting with no guardian seats, and
> `oc-testnet-v1`, a single-key test federation on testnet that orangecheck
> operates. **While it is in testing, orangecheck holds every user's accrued
> balance and is the party that moves it.** That is a stage, deliberately:
> orangecheck operates every role until independent operators take them. Read
> the rest of this page as the destination, and
> [ochk.app/custody](https://ochk.app/custody) as the present tense.

**In the design, independent guardians hold the bitcoin.** A **Fedimint
federation** — an M-of-N set of independent guardians that threshold-sign
together — holds the bitcoin. orangecheck's executor holds your claim on it, as
ecash it can spend, until it pays you out to a Lightning wallet you choose. The
executor sends payouts and holds the key that signs settlements; with a
federation, it pays from the ecash it holds, and a gateway only routes the
payment. None of that is in force: no guardian federation is bound.

If you run a guardian, read the [operator runbook](/me/operator/runbook). If
you're a user, this page is enough.

## What "federation custody" actually means

A Fedimint federation is a small group of independent operators (the
**guardians**) running coordinated software. The federation as a whole holds the
bitcoin and issues ecash notes — bearer-form claims on it. In this design
orangecheck's executor holds that ecash for you, not your browser. Moving the
bitcoin itself takes the guardians' threshold signature.

| If this party          | tries to                      | the result is                                                                                                     |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| orangecheck's executor | spend the ecash it holds      | possible: that is how it pays you out                                                                             |
| orangecheck            | move the federation's bitcoin | possible only with enough seats to reach the threshold; [/custody](https://ochk.app/custody) shows whether it can |
| Fewer than M guardians | move the federation's bitcoin | impossible — threshold not met                                                                                    |
| M+ colluding guardians | move the federation's bitcoin | possible (this is what M-of-N means)                                                                              |
| You                    | get paid out                  | a payout sends your sats to a Lightning wallet you choose                                                         |

How much this leaves with orangecheck depends on **the specific federation**.
Specifically:

1. **At least 4 guardian seats filled**, threshold strictly greater than half
   (so 3-of-4, 4-of-7, etc.). A production federation needs 4, so one guardian
   can fail.
2. **A different operator key in every seat, held by separate legal entities in
   3 or more countries.** Operator-key diversity is the property that counts;
   spreading hosting providers helps with incidents but is not a trust gate.
3. **A published, content-addressed charter, signed by the guardians**, with an
   on-chain way out written into it.
4. **A Lightning gateway run by someone other than orangecheck.**
5. **orangecheck holds no seat.**

[ochk.app/custody](https://ochk.app/custody) checks each of these live, derived
from the seat roster and the signed charter.

## Where to find your federation's charter

Once a federation is bound, the charter (with all of the above disclosed) is
published in three places:

1. The federation's own site / status page.
2. ochk.app's [`/custody`](https://ochk.app/custody) page, including the SHA-256
   hash of the charter so you can verify the copy you read against the one OC
   bound.
3. `https://me.ochk.io/api/operator/charter?federation=<slug>`, with the hash
   and each guardian's signature over it.

Hash mismatch between any of those three is a red flag. If the federation amends
its charter without re-publishing the new hash on ochk.app, tell us at
[ochk.app/contact](https://ochk.app/contact).

## What your browser does

Nothing, for custody. In this design your browser holds no Fedimint wallet and
no mnemonic: orangecheck's executor holds the ecash and pays you out. ochk.app
has no browser-side Fedimint client, and binding a federation would not add one.
See [Current state](#current-state).

## What if a guardian goes down?

The federation continues to operate as long as at least `threshold` of the
guardians are reachable. With a 3-of-4 federation, one guardian can be down
indefinitely without affecting payouts.

If `(N - threshold + 1)` guardians go down at once, the federation itself can't
reach threshold and payouts pause until guardians are restored. This is the
federation's responsibility to communicate.
[`/custody`](https://ochk.app/custody) shows who holds each role and the
federation's trust posture, read live; it does not mirror the federation's
status page.

## What if OC sunsets?

The [oc-me-v1 charter](/federation/oc-me-v1)'s exit clause was written for a
design in which each user held a Fedimint wallet and its mnemonic in the
browser. ochk.app has no such wallet or mnemonic: in this design orangecheck's
executor holds your claim as ecash and pays you out to a Lightning wallet you
choose. Today orangecheck holds what you earn, and no real sats move yet.

## Moving to your own keys

Not open. In this design, your sats reach keys you hold as a payout to a
Lightning wallet you choose;
[`/me/balance`](https://ochk.app/me/balance#own-keys) shows whether that is
open.

Your OC identity stays the same, and so does the `signing_method` on your
`/api/auth/me` payload: it says only whether you sign with your own Bitcoin key,
never where your sats sit. Sat-earning continues unchanged.

## Current state

No guardian federation is bound, and the browser-side Fedimint client that would
have joined one was removed from `oc-me-web` in September 2026 — it could only
ever reach its own error path, and code that demonstrates a money movement which
cannot happen reads as a feature someone forgot to link up. The guardians that
once ran this were torn down in July 2026. Who will run the guardians is not
decided yet.

So today orangecheck holds what you accrue. There is no stored balance to hold,
strictly speaking — it is recomputed from your signed billable envelopes on
every read — but the party who owes it is orangecheck, and that is the honest
way to say it.

We are not soft-pedaling this. [ochk.app/custody](https://ochk.app/custody) says
who holds what today.

Signing in via BIP-322 gives you a real, live self-custody property — your
signing key never reaches OC. It does not make your earned sats self-custodied.
Those are two different questions, and conflating them is the mistake this page
used to make.
